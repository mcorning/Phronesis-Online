**THE PHRONESIS PROJECT**  
**BOARD RESOLUTION**  
**APPOINTMENT OF DIRECTOR**


The undersigned, being the duly elected and acting members of the Board of Directors of The Phronesis Project, a nonprofit corporation organized under the laws of the State of Oregon, hereby adopt the following resolution:

**WHEREAS**, the Bylaws of the corporation authorize the Board of Directors to appoint additional directors; and

**WHEREAS**, the Board has determined that it is in the best interest of the corporation to appoint an additional director;

**NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED**, that **Ross Smith** is hereby appointed to serve as a member of the Board of Directors of The Phronesis Project, effective as of the date of this resolution, to serve in accordance with the Bylaws of the corporation;

**FURTHER RESOLVED**, that the officers of the corporation are authorized and directed to take all actions necessary to reflect this appointment in the corporation’s official records and filings.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the undersigned have executed this resolution as of the date first written above.
